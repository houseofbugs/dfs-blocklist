! DFS Unified AdGuard Home - Custom Allowlist
! ==============================================
! Add global exception rules here when a legitimate domain used by you or your
! clients is blocked by an upstream list (false positive).
!
! Syntax:
!   @@||example.com^$important
!
! Using `$important` ensures the allow rule overrides any upstream block rule
! across all AdGuard Home deployments once compiled and synced.
!
! Examples (uncomment or add lines below):
! @@||client-portal.example.com^$important
! @@||tracking-required-by-app.example.net^$important
! DFS primary company domain
@@||digitalforgesystems.com^$important
! DFS in-house self-hosted RustDesk relay
@@||rustdesk.digitalforgesystems.com^$important
! DFS secondary company domain
@@||dfsys.it^$important
! DFS self-hosted RustDesk WAN IP
@@||184.18.0.169^$important
