! DFS Unified AdGuard Home - Custom DNS Rewrites (Host-to-IP Mapping)
! ======================================================================
! Map or change a hostname to a specific IP address across all AdGuard Home
! deployments using AdGuard's `$dnsrewrite` modifier.
!
! Syntax:
!   ||host.example.com^$dnsrewrite=192.168.1.50
!   ||host.example.com^$dnsrewrite=192.168.1.50,client=192.168.1.0/24
!   ||host.example.com^$dnsrewrite=NOERROR;A;192.168.1.50
!
! Rules in this file are injected directly at the top of `blocklist.txt`
! during compilation so modifiers like `$dnsrewrite` and `$client` are
! always preserved intact.
!
! Examples (uncomment or add lines below):
! ||nas.internal.example^$dnsrewrite=10.0.10.25
! ||printer.office.local^$dnsrewrite=192.168.50.10,client=192.168.50.0/24
